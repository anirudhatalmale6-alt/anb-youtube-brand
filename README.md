## Why the banner looks like that

YouTube shows the whole 2560 x 1440 image on a TV, but on a phone it crops to the
middle 1546 x 423 only. Every word and the logo sit inside that box, so nothing
important is cut off on any device. `AB-banner-safe-area-guide.png` draws the box
so you can see it.

## Channel description to paste in

A&B First Aid Training is a nationally recognised training organisation (RTO 46055)
based in St Marys, NSW. We deliver CPR, First Aid, Childcare First Aid and Advanced
First Aid courses to individuals, workplaces and community groups across Western Sydney.

This channel is for our students and anyone who wants to be more confident in an
emergency - short refreshers on CPR and DRSABCD, walkthroughs of what happens on
course day, and step-by-step help with using our online student portal.

Nationally recognised. Same-day certificates. Book online any time.
